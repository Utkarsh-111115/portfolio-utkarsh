# Asset sources and credits

## Paytm wordmark

The site uses Paytm’s current blue-and-cyan wordmark from the official Investor Relations website header SVG (`https://pwebassets.paytm.com/investorrelations/static/c0f63462..svg`), linked from [Paytm Investor Relations](https://ir.paytm.com/press-kit). The vector has been cropped to the Paytm-only wordmark; its original path shapes and brand colors are unchanged, and the unrelated “Investor Relations” suffix is omitted. Both displayed marks link back to Paytm’s official press kit. The portfolio identifies Utkarsh’s employment accurately and does not imply Paytm endorsement.

## Unsplash photographs

All three photographs are free under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The visual credit on each image links to the photographer and source photo page; the credit is intentionally small and unobtrusive so the photo remains the focus.

- **Production-wallet case study:** Kevin Ache, [A rack of servers in a server room](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-rack-of-servers-in-a-server-room-2JJ3wBHu4_0). Editorial context only; not a depiction of Paytm’s internal environment.
- **Real-time transaction-processing project:** SumUp, [A person holding a credit card at a table](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-person-holding-a-credit-card-at-a-table-eN_DifpOb4Q). A real card-present payment scene; the image is illustrative and does not imply that Paytm uses SumUp hardware.
- **Cloud-based expense tracker:** Kelly Sikkema, [Laptop, papers, calculator, and coffee on blanket](https://unsplash.com/photos/laptop-papers-calculator-and-coffee-on-blanket-kpcGHrYiYKg). A real budgeting-workspace scene, used as editorial project context.
